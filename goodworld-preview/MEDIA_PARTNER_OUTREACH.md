# GoodWorld photo permission workflow
Status: prepared for editorial use, not yet sent. Use an approved GoodWorld sender.

## First request: specific story and a sustainable relationship

Subject: Photo permission request from GoodWorld

Hello [organization] media team,

I'm writing from GoodWorld (https://www.goodworld.news/), an independent positive-news project that shares verified stories from around the world in English and ten other languages.

We would love to cover [specific verified achievement, original announcement URL]. Do you have an original photo of this story that we may use with full credit and a link back to your organization?

If possible, please confirm that you own or are authorized to grant the relevant rights and that the photo may be reproduced worldwide, non-exclusively and without a fee, in our GoodWorld article and its translated editions, archive, story previews and related social posts, including reasonable resizing/cropping. Please let us know the required photographer credit, any restrictions, duration, or subjects' consent limitations.

We would also be happy to hear about future positive achievements from your team. If you have a press distribution list or a media library with clear editorial reuse terms, please point us to it.

Thank you for your work and your time.

GoodWorld Editorial
https://www.goodworld.news/

## Recording permission
- Store the exact photo(s), reply/email reference, sender's authority, scope, expiration and restrictions; no reply means no permission.
- The source of the photograph and the subject's privacy/consent need separate review. A press contact alone never grants rights.
- Do not use personal or unrelated business mailboxes without authorization.
- For supplied photos, ask for a copy or authorized download rather than extracting image links from a press release.
- If an organization wants payment, do not agree or accept any paid service without explicit approval.
