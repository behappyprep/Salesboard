# GoodWorld editorial rule: joy first

Updated 2026-10-30 after founder review of first Asia pilot.

## Product promise
GoodWorld is a place to discover genuinely delightful, heartwarming, surprising TRUE stories that make readers smile and want to share the story with a friend. "Positive" government or corporate press releases are not enough.

## Strong fits
1. **Emotional reunions and acts of kindness.** A lost pet returns home after years; a stranger helps someone fulfill a dream; a village works together to help a neighbor.
2. **Adorable and surprising animals.** Actual births, rescues with a happy outcome, successful adoptions, unlikely animal friendships, concrete wildlife comeback milestones.
3. **Little miracles and joyful human achievements.** Unexpected happy endings, heartwarming discoveries, generous surprises, genuine celebrations of everyday people.
4. **Wonderful discoveries and tangible nature recoveries.** A long-missing species is found alive, a spectacular rare phenomenon is documented, a restored habitat demonstrably flourishes.

## Automatic exclusion
- Government/ministerial announcements, officials' speeches, bills, plans, policies, grants, financial commitments, construction proposals, conferences, trade fairs, PR launches, AI tool announcements, general technology development, corporate promises.
- Sports medal statistics by themselves; only a specific exceptional human story with a compelling happy outcome might qualify.
- Disaster, tragedy, war, fear, crime or controversy as the lead, even when a related policy response is described positively.
- Generic feel-good claims, speculative benefits or vague 'could help someday' stories.
- Stories without a verified *completed* happy outcome.
- Syndicated or third-party articles whose translation rights are not independently confirmed.

## Editorial acceptance test
Ask: "Would an ordinary person anywhere share this with a friend and say: 'Look how wonderful!'?"
Score each individually from 0 to 5:
- **Emotional impact and warmth**
- **Surprise / genuine delight**
- **Easy to understand across languages**
- **Concrete and verified positive outcome**
- **Sharing potential**
A normal candidate needs at least **21/25** and at least 4/5 for emotional impact; otherwise reject. No fixed quota; a day with 0 stories is better than boring filler.

## Legal and editorial safeguards
- Verify that the particular original text is reusable and translatable commercially, including terms and any exceptions for third-party content. Keep source URL and original author.
- Attribute the original author prominently, link original source and actual license, and disclose GoodWorld translation. Do not describe a translation as original GoodWorld reporting.
- Never assume photos are licensed with the text; verify each photo separately. Use GoodWorld fallback illustration when permission is missing.
- Keep queue private and unapproved until all checks pass. Editorial review determines quality, *not* keyword matches.
- For RSS sources that lack article detail, do not invent a happy ending.
- Deduplicate the original story and avoid 'one press release = one published news card' mentality.

## Asia pilot diagnosis
Oct 30: Government feeds from Japan's Digital Agency and Taiwan's Executive Yuan returned 84 entries, but the 10 keyword-selected entries were infrastructure, policy and official updates. All 10 were rejected following founder review. A strict outcome-first screening pass over the same feeds returned 0 eligible entries. This is not an error: sources are poor matches for the product promise.

## Better source strategy
Prefer original local-language firsthand stories on animals, rescues, community kindness and surprising good events. Rights-cleared stories must have actual republishing/translation permission. Global Voices offers CC BY for its own content but is often a translation of another language, so inspect original reporting language, third-party assets and story appeal; do not assume every Global Voices story is locally original. For closed-licensed outlets seek written syndication/translation permission, or create a genuinely independent fact-based report without copying expression, subject to legal review.
