ABOUT GOODWORLD

Good news. Verified sources.

GoodWorld is an independent, free-to-read collection of positive developments in science, nature, animal welfare, healthcare and human kindness.

What we publish

We look for concrete outcomes, not just promises. Stories come from identifiable sources such as national parks, scientific institutions, public health regulators and wildlife organisations. Each story links back to the original publisher, so you can read the full context yourself.

GoodWorld publishes concise summaries and occasional longer editorial articles. We are not the original publisher of the underlying announcements, and summaries are not substitutes for the original reporting.

How publication works

Our collection, scheduling, and photo-licensing checks use automation. News is queued and published throughout the day. Publication on GoodWorld does not mean an event happened today. The original source date and linked source are the best reference for the timing of each event.

We do not manufacture event photography. Where a licensed photograph illustrates the subject rather than the specific event, we label it “Representative photo” and credit its creator and license. Medical and scientific claims should be checked against the primary source.

Eleven languages

Our English content is also available through automated translations. These translations have not all been reviewed by a native-speaking editor. For important medical, scientific or regulatory details, refer to the English original and the linked primary source.

Corrections are welcome

See something inaccurate, outdated or misattributed? Please send a correction. We take source accuracy and photographic rights seriously.
