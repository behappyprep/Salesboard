Human Kindness · October 21, 2026

He found $128,000 by an ATM. He returned it all.

A Texas passerby spotted bags of cash by a bank machine and called the police instead of taking the money.

A remarkable moment of honesty unfolded in Lewisville, Texas, when a person found bags containing more than $128,000 near an ATM. Rather than walk away with the money, the finder contacted local police so it could be returned. The story is a reminder that small personal decisions can have enormous consequences for other people.
