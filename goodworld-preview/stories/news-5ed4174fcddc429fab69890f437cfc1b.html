A wildlife bridge is helping make a Colorado highway safer.

Early data from a large wildlife crossing on Interstate 25 show a reported 91% fall in animal-vehicle collisions.

Colorado built a major wildlife overpass above Interstate 25 near Larkspur so animals could cross without stepping into traffic. About ten months after opening, early monitoring reported 91% fewer wildlife-vehicle collisions in the affected area. Wildlife fencing and nearby underpasses complement the bridge. The result suggests that well-designed crossings can protect both wildlife and drivers.
