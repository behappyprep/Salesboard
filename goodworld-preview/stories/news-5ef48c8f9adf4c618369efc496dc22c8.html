Mozambique protects a forest known for its butterflies.

Mount Mabu's newly designated conservation area covers about 23,000 acres and safeguards a habitat rich in rare species.

Mozambique has designated around 23,000 acres around Mount Mabu as a community conservation area. The mountain's isolated forests are home to remarkable biodiversity, including more than 200 butterfly species in the wider mountain region. Formal protection is an important step toward preserving habitats that researchers continue to explore and document.
