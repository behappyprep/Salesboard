Strangers came together to free an owl caught in a fence.

A couple spotted an injured owl tangled in barbed wire in South Dakota, starting a community rescue effort.

A drive near Spearfish, South Dakota, took an unexpected turn when Sharon and Scott Jacobs noticed a bird trapped on a barbed-wire fence. The bird was a great horned owl in need of urgent help. The couple and others organized a rescue, and the injured bird was taken for professional rehabilitation. Quick attention and teamwork gave the owl a chance to recover.
