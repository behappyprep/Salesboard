Human Kindness · October 21, 2026

A student staged a fake cafeteria fight. It was a birthday surprise.

Teenagers in Georgia turned a school principal's worst moment into a celebration organized by a student he had helped.

At a Georgia high school, principal Bobby Davis rushed to the cafeteria after hearing there was a fight. Instead, he walked into a carefully planned birthday surprise. The student behind the prank, De'Zayviun Watkins, credits Davis with helping him redirect his life and manage anger. What looked like trouble turned into a heartfelt public thank-you.
