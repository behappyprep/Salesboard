Human Kindness · October 21, 2026

A couple facing a tough winter received a $16,000 gift.

A heating and cooling company surprised Oregon homeowners with a complete replacement system at no cost.

Diane and Brian Johnsen of Beaverton, Oregon, faced a difficult winter when their old heating system could no longer be repaired. Through a community initiative involving Lennox and local contractor Aviator Heating & Cooling, they received a new heating and cooling installation valued at roughly $16,000. The gift eased a financial burden and brought new comfort to their home.
