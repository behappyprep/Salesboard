A 700-year-old mural inspires a remarkable cross-border collaboration.

Chinese artists worked with New York's Metropolitan Museum of Art to reproduce a historic Buddhist mural in painstaking detail.

A team of Chinese artists spent years studying and carefully reproducing a monumental Buddhist mural displayed at the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York. With cooperation from the museum, the researchers documented its cracks, colors and history while creating a full-scale replica. The project shows how cultural institutions can collaborate across borders to preserve and better understand shared artistic heritage.
