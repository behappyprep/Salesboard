More than 1,000 bison now roam an expanding prairie reserve.

American Prairie's conservation project has reached a milestone: its free-roaming bison herd now numbers in the four figures.

In the northern Great Plains, conservation nonprofit American Prairie is restoring connected grasslands and expanding room for wildlife. The organization reports that its bison herd has surpassed 1,000 for the first time. It is one milestone in a larger, ongoing effort to reconnect habitats and help native species return to landscapes they once dominated.
