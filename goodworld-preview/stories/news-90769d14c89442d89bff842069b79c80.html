An overweight rescue bulldog gets healthier and finds a home.

Peggy Sue arrived at an English animal center unable to move comfortably. Care and a healthier routine brought a fresh start.

When Peggy Sue arrived at the RSPCA's West Hatch Animal Centre in Somerset, England, the bulldog weighed around 70 pounds and struggled with everyday movement. Shelter workers helped her gradually regain mobility through careful attention and a healthier routine. The organization reports that Peggy Sue also found a new home, making the transformation about much more than weight.
