Jersey expands protection to more than a fifth of its seas.

New rules protect about 21.7% of Jersey's waters from damaging seabed fishing practices.

Jersey, in the English Channel, has expanded the network of marine areas protected against practices such as bottom trawling. Roughly 21.7% of its surrounding waters will now fall under these protections, according to reporting on the new regulations. Safeguarding vulnerable seafloor habitats can help marine ecosystems recover and provide nursery areas for fish and other wildlife.
