Aldabra's giant tortoises are thriving: about 180,000 counted.

A new survey estimates roughly 180,000 giant tortoises live on the Aldabra atoll in the Seychelles.

The remote Aldabra atoll in the Seychelles is home to one of the world's greatest giant tortoise populations. A large survey estimates approximately 180,000 tortoises across the islands, an encouraging result for long-term conservation. Protecting island ecosystems helps these exceptionally long-lived animals survive through generations.
