Seventeen years after his rescue, this sloth bear is flourishing.

Odum, a sloth bear saved from the dancing-bear trade in India, is doing well at a sanctuary years after his rescue.

Odum was rescued in India in 2009 from a life in which he had been forced to perform for tourists. Actor Julia Roberts later visited the sanctuary and helped give him his name. New updates from animal welfare organizations describe the bear as flourishing. His story highlights how much sustained rehabilitation, care and protection can mean for an animal rescued from abuse.
