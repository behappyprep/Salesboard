Animals · October 21, 2026

A tiny kitten hitchhiked in a car. It found a family.

A couple in Georgia called rescuers after discovering a black kitten stuck under their car. They ended up adopting him.

Nicole and Brandon were driving in Sandy Springs, Georgia, when another motorist alerted them to something underneath their vehicle. Firefighters and police helped free a kitten trapped in the suspension. The couple took the little cat for veterinary care, then welcomed him into their home and named him Friday Shadow. A frightening journey became a happy new beginning.
