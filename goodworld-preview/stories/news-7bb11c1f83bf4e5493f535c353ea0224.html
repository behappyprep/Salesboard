At 99, she took to the skies on top of a plane.

Helga Duffy was recognized by Guinness World Records for a wing-walking adventure at the age of 99.

Helga Duffy chose an extraordinary new adventure at 99: standing secured to the wing of a small aircraft in flight. After a stunt at an airfield in Gloucestershire, England, Guinness World Records recognized her achievement as the oldest female wing-walker. Her story is a joyful reminder that curiosity and courage are not limited to the young.
