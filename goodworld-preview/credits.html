EDITORIAL TRANSPARENCY

Sources & photo credits.

We keep news cards uncluttered without hiding the origin of a story or a photograph.

What the small links mean

Source ↗ opens the original news report. The full name of the publisher and the source link also appear on each story page.

© Name ↗ links to the original photograph or photographer's credit page. It identifies the credited creator or rights holder, not GoodWorld.

CC BY / CC BY-SA / other license ↗ links to the photo's licensing or rights information. Conditions differ between licenses. The license link should be consulted before any reuse.

Representative photographs

A photograph labeled Representative photo illustrates the subject, not necessarily the specific event. Cropping for the website's layout is indicated in the detailed article photo credit where applicable. We do not present illustrative photographs as documentation of an event.

Find the full details

Open any story to see its original publisher, linked report, photographer credit and image-license link when a photograph is displayed. The source links and photo credits on the small news card remain directly clickable.

Corrections

If a credit, license, date or attribution is incorrect, please contact our editorial team.
